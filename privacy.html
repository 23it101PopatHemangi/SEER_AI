SEER AI – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

SEER AI is an academic software project developed for educational and project purposes. This Privacy Policy explains how SEER AI handles information when a user connects a Jira account to the application.

1. Information We Access

When you authorize SEER AI to connect to Jira, the application may access information from your Jira account that is necessary for the features you use, such as Jira account information, projects, issues, and related Jira data permitted by the OAuth scopes configured for the application.

2. How We Use Information

Information accessed through Jira is used only to provide and demonstrate SEER AI's Jira-related functionality, such as displaying or working with Jira project and issue information.

3. Data Sharing

SEER AI does not sell personal information. Information accessed from Jira is not intentionally shared with third parties except where necessary to provide the application's requested functionality or where required by law.

4. Data Storage and Security

SEER AI uses reasonable technical measures to protect application data. The application is an academic project, and users should avoid providing sensitive or confidential information that is not required for the project's functionality.

5. Atlassian Data

Jira data is accessed through Atlassian's OAuth 2.0 authorization process. SEER AI only requests the permissions required for the application's implemented features.

6. User Choices

You may stop using the Jira integration and revoke the application's access through the relevant Atlassian account settings. You may also contact the project team regarding questions about data handled by SEER AI.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the functionality or data practices of SEER AI change. The latest version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions about this academic project, contact the SEER AI project team at the email address provided by the project team.
